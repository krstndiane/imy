FAQ

Is this actually them? +

No. IMY imitates how they write, based on what you give it. It can’t know what they think or feel, and it never contacts them.

Which chat apps can it look like? +

Messenger, Instagram DMs, iMessage, WhatsApp and Telegram, each in light or dark.

How many screenshots do I need? +

Around ten gives it enough to pick up their tone. You can also paste chat text, which keeps emojis.

Is this a replacement for talking to someone? +

No. It’s a place to say what you need to say. If missing someone is weighing on you, a friend or a counsellor can help in ways an app can’t.
